# Taglines

Ranked within each group by how well they survive a stranger reading them once,
with no context, on a phone.

## The house line

**Are you BotReady?**

It is the product name as a question, it is on the site, and it is what people
will repeat. Everything below sits underneath it rather than replacing it.

## Cleared for the headline slot

Each of these is a statement of fact about a real scan result, which is why they
are allowed to be the biggest words on the page.

1. **200 for Chrome. 403 for ClaudeBot. Same second.**
   The whole product in nine words. Use it wherever there is room for three
   lines. Product Hunt gallery 1, the launch OG card, the X card, the LinkedIn
   card, the 336×280.

2. **Your site is fine. Your site is also invisible.**
   For the audience that has never considered this could be a problem, because
   nothing is broken. Instagram, LinkedIn, the Google display line.

3. **The 403 you did not mean to send.**
   For engineers. It presumes the reader knows what a 403 is and respects them
   for it. X, Product Hunt comments, the /bot page.

4. **Half the clients that answer questions about you cannot read you.**
   The longest one that still works as a headline. Press, Substack.

5. **Find out what the agents see.**
   The safe one. Google search ads, where you get thirty characters and no
   room to be clever.

  It happened anyway.
- It is free, it takes thirty seconds, and you can stop reading this if it comes
  back green.

## Retired, and why

Kept so nobody re-proposes them.

- ~~"Get cited by AI"~~ — we cannot promise citation and will not imply we can.
- ~~"Rank #1 in ChatGPT"~~ — there is no rank. Saying so is the grift we are
  differentiating from.
- ~~"AI SEO for the agentic era"~~ — three pieces of jargon, no claim.
- ~~"Don't get left behind"~~ — fear with nothing behind it. Our whole pitch is
  that the thing is checkable, so check it.
- ~~"Unlock your AI visibility"~~ — "unlock" is banned in the voice rules.
