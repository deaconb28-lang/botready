# Instagram

The hardest fit of the eight channels, so the brief is narrow: this is where the
design system does the work. Nobody is going to run a scan from Instagram. What
Instagram does is make an infrastructure product look like a thing a person
made, which is what gets it reposted into a founder's story and seen by the four
people who will actually use it.

Grid rule: alternate a **statement frame** (huge type on lavender, one line) with
an **artefact frame** (a real screenshot or a drawn UI). Never two statements in
a row — it reads as a quote account.

Everything here is generated by `assets/build.mjs` at 1080×1080 and 1080×1920.

**Caption**
```
Three ways this happens and not one of them is a decision.

A bot protection preset that cannot tell a scraper from a client trying to
answer a question about you. A robots.txt blocklist pasted off a forum by
somebody who has since left. A firewall rule written at 2am after a scraping
incident and never removed.

All three produce the same 403. None of them was in a meeting.

And you cannot find it by looking harder at the tools you have — a refused
request never becomes a session, so it is not in analytics, and Search Console
is green because Googlebot went through fine.

The check is three curl commands, or thirty seconds at botready.dev. Free,
no account, nothing blurred.

#webdev #seo #ai #cloudflare #devtools #buildinpublic
```

## Reels

Three, 15 to 25 seconds each, screen recordings with no music and no captions
burned in. The product is the content.

**Reel 1 — the split.** Terminal, full screen, large type. Type the Chrome curl,
enter, `200` in lime. Type the ClaudeBot curl, enter, `403` in coral. Hold on
both for two seconds. Cut to the URL box. No voiceover.

**Reel 2 — the scan.** Screen recording of a real scan, real time, unsped. Paste
a URL, the live page fills, the result lands, the score counts up. Thirty
seconds is the whole point — speeding it up would be the first dishonest frame.

**Reel 3 — the fix pack.** Open the fix pack, click through the five files,
scroll the llms.txt, land on the coding-agent prompt. Caption on screen: "$15.
Generated from your own scan."

## Captions, general rules

- The first line is the whole hook. Everything after "more" is for the two people
  who care.
- Hashtags at the bottom, six at most, all real communities:
  `#webdev #devtools #seo #buildinpublic #frontend #ai`
- Never write "link in bio" as the only call to action. Say what happens if they
  click.
- No stock photography, ever. If there is no artefact to show, it is a statement
  frame in the design system.
