# The moving assets

Everything in `marketing/` argues one sentence. This directory is that sentence
with a clock attached: a narrated launch film, its cutdowns, and the silent
real-time recordings `../schedule.md` asks for at T-9.

Read `../README.md` first. The rules there are not softened by being on video.

## Nothing here is a picture of the product

Every frame that shows BotReady is a recording of BotReady running. The title
cards are drawn in a real Chromium from the real `apps/web/app/tokens.css` and
the real TTFs in `apps/web/assets/fonts`, so a card and a screen recording are
the same pixels rather than a near-match. No frame of this film was generated by
an image or video model, and no person appears in it who does not exist.

That is not squeamishness. A product that sells a measurement cannot advertise
with a picture of a measurement it did not take, and the first person to notice
would be exactly the engineer we are trying to convince.

The one thing that is synthesised is the narration. It is `text2speech_v2` on
its `elevenlabs` engine, voice preset Emmett, and the choice is recorded as
`VOICE` in `script.mjs` rather than living in somebody's shell history — a line
regenerated on a different engine would not match the other eleven.

If you would rather read the script yourself, drop your takes into `vo/` as
`<beat-id>.mp3` and re-run the build. Nothing else changes, the edit re-times
itself off your durations, and the credit cost of this directory goes to zero.

## What is in here

| | |
|---|---|
| `script.mjs` | **The locked script.** Beats, narration, cutdown definitions, reels. One source of truth for the voice and the edit. |
| `titles.mjs` | Draws the title cards in Chromium from the design tokens |
| `build-film.mjs` | The edit. ffmpeg, driven from Node |
| `footage/` | Recordings from `tools/capture-footage.mjs`, plus `shots.json` |
| `titles/` | Rendered cards, plus `titles.json` |
| `vo/` | One mp3 per narrated beat, named by beat id |
| `out/` | Renders. Build output, not source — git ignores it |

## Building it

```
pnpm --filter @botready/web dev                 # or point --base at production
node tools/capture-footage.mjs --base http://127.0.0.1:3000
node marketing/video/titles.mjs
node marketing/video/build-film.mjs
```

Each step takes `--only` for one shot, card or target. `build-film.mjs` needs no
network; the first step does.

`ffmpeg-static` is a dependency of the `tools` workspace and is in
`pnpm-workspace.yaml`'s `onlyBuiltDependencies`, because pnpm blocks the
postinstall that fetches the binary otherwise. Playwright's own bundled ffmpeg
cannot be used: it is a `--disable-everything` build with no h264 and no audio
encoders at all.

A cutdown names a hard `target` in `script.mjs`. The builder spreads whatever
slack is left across its beats in proportion to their length — dumping it all on
the last beat left the end card holding for two thirds of a short spot — and
refuses to build a cutdown whose beats already overrun, telling you to drop one.
Speeding the read up to fit is not an option the tool offers, on purpose.

The cutdowns are re-cuts from the same beats, never a trim of the master — a trim
lands mid-sentence and mid-animation.

## The 30-second cutdown is not the Product Hunt demo

`../product-hunt.md` is explicit about gallery slot 2:

> No music, no captions, real time — the scan takes thirty seconds and pretending
> otherwise is a lie the first user catches.

`launch-30-16x9.mp4` is a narrated advert. It goes on X, LinkedIn and Google. It
does not go in slot 2, and swapping one for the other is the exact dishonesty
this campaign sells against.

## The one gap

**Reel 2 and the Product Hunt demo are not built, and cannot be built from this
repository alone.** Both are a recording of a scan completing in real time. The
scan page is real: `apps/web/app/scan/live/LiveScan.tsx` polls `/api/scan/:id`
every 1200ms and proves each of its five stages by a specific check landing,
never by a timer. That is the right design and it is why the shot cannot be
faked — with no Supabase, no Redis and no worker, no check ever lands.

`footage/live-open.mp4` is the honest half: the page as it opens, which is real,
and which the film uses. What is missing is the progression.

To finish it, run the harness against an environment with a working backend:

```
node tools/capture-footage.mjs --base https://www.botready.dev --only live-open
```

and extend that shot to drive a real scan — fill the hero form, wait on the log,
stop when it lands. Scan something you own. `../README.md`: *never post a scan
result of a named site without asking the owner first.*

Two things to know before you try:

- A browser in a sandboxed session may not be able to reach the live site at all;
  in the session that built this, Chromium could not open any external host,
  which is why every shot here was captured against `next dev`.
- The scan endpoint is rate limited to 5/hr for anonymous callers
  (`apps/web/lib/site.ts`) and results cache for 24h. Budget the retakes.

## The script's own rules

- No line states a number that is not on screen in the shot beside it. The
  landing-page example shows three of five refused; the fixture shows four. Both
  are said, each over its own frame, and they are never averaged into one claim.
- Nothing claims lost traffic, and nothing claims a score causes citation.
- Both moat lines are in: the weights are published, and we never work around a
  block.
- The score beat carries no narration. The count-up and the six bars filling are
  the beat, and the read was 87 seconds with a line over it.
